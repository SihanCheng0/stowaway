## Checkpoint before sleep

When a safeguard (timer, battery or heat) is about to put a closed Mac to sleep, Stowaway now keeps it awake a little longer so your agents can save their work. That's 60 seconds, 20 seconds if the Mac is hot, and no wait if it's critically hot. The menu bar counts down. It's on by default, under **Safeguards**.

- **Claude Code:** click **Add to Claude Code** and every session is told once to finish up, snapshot uncommitted work, write a note of what's done and what's next, and stop. The hook prints nothing the rest of the time. Your old `settings.json` is kept as a backup.
- **Anything else:** put a script at `~/.config/stowaway/before-sleep`. It runs at the start of the window, with the reason and deadline in its environment. Other tools can watch for `~/.config/stowaway/sleep-pending.json`.

Details and an example script are in the [README](https://github.com/SihanCheng0/stowaway#checkpoint-before-sleep).
